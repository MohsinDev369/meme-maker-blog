The question attached to it, the one that gives our template its long name, is the one Berserk fans have argued about for years: in your opinion, will Guts be truly happy when he finally kills Griffith?

## Where Guts and Berserk come from

*Berserk* is a manga by Kentaro Miura. According to [Wikipedia's Berserk article](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Berserk_(manga)), it began on August 25, 1989, in Hakusensha's *Monthly Animal House* and moved to *Young Animal* in 1992. It has passed 70 million copies in circulation worldwide.

Guts is the lead, a mercenary with a giant sword called the Dragon Slayer. In the Golden Age arc he fights alongside Griffith and the Band of the Hawk, and for a while it's the closest thing he has to a family. Then Griffith betrays the entire band during an event called the Eclipse. Guts survives, but loses his left arm and his right eye, and Casca, the woman he loves, is left deeply traumatized. From then on he is known as the Black Swordsman, and revenge on Griffith is the main thing keeping him alive.

That's the setup for the meme. Guts has spent decades of real publishing time chasing one goal. The obvious question is whether getting it would make him happy at all.

## Where the template image comes from

The template uses a single portrait of Guts rather than a full manga page. It doesn't carry an artist credit or a chapter number, so this post won't guess at one. What matters for the meme is the expression. Most famous Guts images show him screaming, bleeding or swinging the sword. This one shows him quietly relieved, and the contrast is the whole appeal.

On Meme0 the template is listed as "Beserk-tired," which also fits. He looks like someone who is done fighting, at least for the afternoon.

## The happy ending debate behind the meme

Berserk fans have wondered for a long time whether the series could end well for Guts. A lot of that hope comes from a 2000 interview Miura gave to Yukari Fujimoto. As reported by [Epic Dope](https://www.epicdope.com/does-guts-die-in-berserk-what-happens-to-him/), Miura said a happy ending was possible and that he didn't think he could close such a long, grim story on a grim note. He also said he would leave the actual ending to his subconscious.

Fans have read a lot into that answer, probably more than it says. "Possible" isn't a promise.

The debate got heavier in 2021. Miura died on May 6, 2021, at 54, and Hakusensha announced the news on May 20. His last chapter came out that September. In June 2022, Hakusensha and Kouji Mori, a friend of Miura's, announced the series would continue, with Studio Gaga handling the art. New chapters started appearing that same month.

So the question in the template has real weight behind it. My read: the story keeps giving Guts people to protect, which makes revenge look less like the finish line every year. The smiling Guts image lets people play with that idea without writing an essay.

## How people use the "Guts truly happy" format

The format mostly runs on contrast. You pair a long struggle with a small reward, and Guts's calm face sells it.

**Small victories after big suffering.** "Me after finally finding the bug I spent three days on." "Me when the group chat stops arguing." The bigger the setup, the funnier the quiet smile.

**Mock answers to the fan question.** Some people caption it with a fake answer to "will Guts be happy," like "Guts after a warm meal and eight hours of sleep." It's a joke about how little it would take.

**Wholesome Guts.** Berserk fans love the rare soft moments in the series, and this image gets used for those: Guts with his companions, Guts sitting still, Guts not swinging a sword for one panel.

**Paired with sad Berserk memes.** There's a well-known Berserk-adjacent format built around the caption "I don't wanna be horny anymore, I just wanna be happy," usually shown over tired, somber characters. ## Captioning tips

- Put the struggle in the caption and leave the peace to his face.
- Keep text off the center of the image. His expression is small and easy to cover.
- Grey clouds sit at the top of the frame, so white text with a dark outline reads well there.
- Go easy on spoilers for recent chapters. A general joke about Guts suffering works for everyone, a plot detail only works for people who are caught up.

## FAQ

### What is the will Guts be truly happy meme?

It's a Berserk meme built around a calm, smiling portrait of Guts and the long-running fan question of whether killing Griffith would actually make him happy. People caption it with small wins after long struggles.

### Does Guts get a happy ending in Berserk?

Nobody knows yet. Miura said in a 2000 interview that a happy ending was possible, but he died in 2021 before finishing the story. The series continues under Kouji Mori and Studio Gaga, and the ending hasn't been published.

### Who continued Berserk after Kentaro Miura died?

Kouji Mori, a friend of Miura's, supervises the story, and Studio Gaga handles the art. The continuation was announced in June 2022.
