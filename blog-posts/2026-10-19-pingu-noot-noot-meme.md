The Pingu noot noot meme comes from a children's show about a small clay penguin who couldn't speak a real language. Pingu talked in babble, and when he wanted attention he pushed his beak out into a kind of megaphone and honked "noot noot." That sound outlived the show's original run by decades. Today it shows up as a reaction sound, a sticker, and the setup for one of the strangest mood-swing formats on TikTok.

Here's where it all came from and how people use it now.

## Where Pingu comes from

*Pingu* started in Switzerland. Otmar Gutmann and Erika Brueggemann created it, and according to [Wikipedia's Pingu article](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Pingu), a seven-minute pilot was finished in 1986. It screened at the Berlin Film Festival in 1987 and won an award there, which led Swiss television to order a full series.

That series aired on the Swiss channel SF DRS from March 1990 to April 2000. Every episode was stop-motion, with clay and plasticine puppets animated a frame at a time at a studio in Russikon, Switzerland. Pingu lives at the South Pole with his parents and his little sister Pinga, and the penguin families there live and work in igloos. The episodes are about five minutes long.

The show never needed translating. All the characters speak an invented gibberish that fans call "Penguinese." In the original four series, every voice came from one Italian actor, Carlo Bonomi.

## The British side of the story

Pingu is often called a British-Swiss show, and here is why. In October 2001, the British company HIT Entertainment bought the rights. HIT produced a revival with the UK studio Hot Animation, and those new episodes aired on CBeebies from 2003 to 2006. The original clay puppets had worn out by then, so the revival used resin casts of them. Bonomi was replaced by David Sant and Marcello Magni.

There's more after that. Mattel bought HIT in 2012. Japan got a computer-animated spin-off, *Pingu in the City*, which premiered on NHK in October 2017. And in October 2024, Aardman Animations and Mattel Television announced a new stop-motion version that is still in development.

## How "noot noot" became a meme

The sound is the hook. It's short, it's silly, and it comes with a very clear visual, the beak stretched into a trumpet. According to Know Your Meme's [noot noot entry](https://knowyourmeme.com/memes/noot-noot), clips of Pingu were already being remixed in YouTube Poop videos around 2008, but the phrase itself took off online around 2011. After that, "noot noot" turned into general internet shorthand for Pingu, or for any penguin, or just for announcing yourself loudly.

Two things helped it stick.

The first was *Pingu's The Thing*, a 2012 fan film by animator Lee Hardcastle that mashed Pingu up with John Carpenter's horror movie *The Thing*. It went viral when it came out and kept resurfacing in later years. It also set a pattern: Pingu's cute clay world plus something dark is funny.

The second was the terrified Pingu clip.

## The terrified noot noot meme

In May 2022, a short animation of Pingu went viral. He does his usual "noot noot," then freezes and stares into the distance with a horrified face while the "Lacrimosa" from Mozart's *Requiem* plays. Know Your Meme credits the original upload to a YouTuber named Telepurte and notes that it wasn't official Pingu footage.

TikTok turned it into a two-part template. The first half is happy: Pingu honks, and the caption sets up something nice. The second half is the dread: the choir kicks in and the caption reveals the bad part. A Peruvian outlet, [La República](https://larepublica.pe/tendencias/historias/2022/05/27/pinguino-noot-noot-impactado-que-significa-y-por-que-se-ha-vuelto-el-meme-del-momento-viral-tiktok-tendencia), described it the same way in late May 2022: a cheerful noot, then a cut to the penguin stunned by something tragic, with users stitching both halves into a little story.

Typical setups look like this:

- "Me opening my bank app after the weekend"
- "When the teacher says the test is open book" / "...and it's a math test"
- "Finally getting the house to myself" / "hearing a noise downstairs"

## FAQ

### What does "noot noot" mean?

It's the honking sound Pingu makes by stretching his beak into a horn shape. It doesn't mean anything specific in the show's made-up language. Online it's used as a greeting, a sound effect, or a way to say "Pingu."

### Is Pingu Swiss or British?

Both. The original series (1990 to 2000) was made in Switzerland. The later revival (2003 to 2006) was produced in the UK for HIT Entertainment and aired on CBeebies.

### What is the terrified noot noot meme?

It's a 2022 fan-made clip where Pingu says "noot noot" and then stares in horror while Mozart's "Lacrimosa" plays. On TikTok it became a two-part format: a happy setup followed by a dark twist.
