Jujutsu Kaisen memes have a strange split personality. The series is brutal, characters die constantly, and yet the fandom treats half the cast like sitcom regulars. Choso is the overprotective big brother. Higuruma is the tired office worker. Gojo is the guy who talks a big game. This guide explains where each of those Jujutsu Kaisen meme formats comes from and how people use them.

## A quick background on Jujutsu Kaisen

Gege Akutami wrote and drew the manga, which ran in Shueisha's Weekly Shonen Jump from March 5, 2018 to September 30, 2024, and finished at 30 volumes. MAPPA handles the anime. Season 1 aired from October 2020 to March 2021, Season 2 covered Hidden Inventory and the Shibuya Incident in the second half of 2023, and Season 3, The Culling Game Part 1, ran from January 9 to March 27, 2026. [Wikipedia's series page](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Jujutsu_Kaisen) has the full broadcast history.

That Season 3 window matters for memes. It brought Higuruma to the anime for the first time, and anime-only fans picked him up fast.

## The Choso crying meme

Choso is one of the Death Paintings, cursed womb beings created by Kenjaku. He is the eldest of nine, and his whole personality is built around his younger brothers. He first comes at Yuji Itadori looking for revenge during the Shibuya Incident, then realizes mid-fight that Yuji is his half-brother and switches sides. After that he basically appoints himself Yuji's guardian.

The [Choso crying template](https://www.meme0.com/memes/choso-crying) is a black and white manga panel. Choso is in profile, tears running down his face, mouth set in a tight, wobbly line. He looks like someone trying very hard to hold it together and losing.

That restraint is what makes it useful. People use it for:

- Proud-sibling moments. "When your little brother finally learns to cook rice without burning it."
- Small wins that feel huge. Passing a driving test, a bug finally fixed, the group chat remembering your birthday.
- Fandom grief, usually about a character in the series. JJK fans have no shortage of material for that.

The joke usually depends on contrast. A tiny, harmless thing on the caption side, a man crying like he just watched his family's history flash before his eyes on the image side. Since Choso is the self-declared big brother of the series, sibling captions work best.

## Higuruma memes: the lawyer who looks tired of everything

Hiromi Higuruma is a former defense lawyer who lost faith in the justice system after watching it fail. He picks up cursed abilities during the Culling Game. His Domain Expansion, Deadly Sentencing, turns the fight into a trial. A shikigami called Judgeman presides, violence is banned inside the domain, and the target has to answer a charge.

In the anime, his trial against Yuji is in Season 3 Episode 9, "Tokyo No. 1 Colony Part 3." [Outlook Respawn's recap](https://respawn.outlookindia.com/pop-culture/pop-culture-originals/jujutsu-kaisen-season-3-episode-9-review-recap) walks through it: Yuji is first found guilty of going into a pachinko parlor while underage, then the retrial charges him over the Shibuya Incident. A life-or-death fight that opens with a pachinko charge is about as JJK as it gets.

Meme0 has two Higuruma templates, both from the manga:

- [Hiromi Higuruma stressed out](https://www.meme0.com/memes/hiromi-higuruma-stressed-out-meme), where he presses his hands together over his face and stares up and off to the side with wide, exhausted eyes. It is pure "I need a second."
- [Hiromi thinking](https://www.meme0.com/memes/hiromi-thinking-meme), where he leans on his clasped hands and gives a sideways look. It reads as quiet judgment.

Higuruma memes lean into the adult-at-work angle. He looks like someone who has read too many case files. Typical captions:

- "Me reading the email that says 'per my last email.'"
- "Teacher when the whole class says the homework was not on the syllabus."
- The thinking panel with a caption like "So what you are telling me is..." before a ridiculous excuse.

A March 2026 piece on [Afisha.uz](https://www.afisha.uz/uz/cinema/2026/03/03/higuruma) also pointed out that a yawning scene of his spread on social media as a way to complain about work and school. That fits the same lane. Higuruma is the patron saint of being done with it.

## Gojo-era jokes and "Nah, I'd win"

Satoru Gojo's confidence has been a fandom running joke for years. The biggest Gojo meme is "Nah, I'd win."

It comes from Chapter 221, which went live on VIZ on April 23, 2023. Asked if he could lose to Sukuna, Gojo answers with the line, which also echoes something he told Yuji much earlier. According to [meme.com's breakdown](https://meme.com/memes/nah-id-win), scanlations translated it three different ways, VIZ updated its own version to "Nah, I'd win" to match the earlier callback, and the format took off from there. Fans started rewriting the speech bubble and pasting other characters over Gojo, and the meme peaked in November 2023.

The spoiler part: Gojo does not get the result that line promises. That is why it became funny instead of just cool. Today people use it two ways:

- As a catchphrase for overconfidence. "Do you think you can finish this project by Friday?" "Nah, I'd win."
- As a template, where someone swaps in a different character, often a weak one, to make the confidence look even more ridiculous.

Gojo-era jokes in general play on that gap between how unbeatable he looks and how things actually go. If you pair the Choso crying template with a Gojo caption, you get the fandom's mood in one image.

## Why JJK memes work so well

The series swings between horror and goofy banter, sometimes in the same chapter. That gives fans permission to joke about it. Plenty of characters also have very clear single-trait personalities in fan culture, like Choso the brother and Higuruma the jaded lawyer, which makes them easy to cast in everyday situations.

## FAQ

### Why is Choso crying a meme?

Choso is known for being intensely devoted to his younger brothers, including Yuji, whom he discovers is his half-brother. The crying panel shows him holding back tears, so fans use it for proud-sibling moments and small wins that feel overwhelming.

### What episode is Higuruma's trial against Yuji?

In the anime it is Jujutsu Kaisen Season 3 Episode 9, "Tokyo No. 1 Colony Part 3," part of The Culling Game Part 1, which aired from January to March 2026.

### What chapter is "Nah, I'd win" from?

It is from Jujutsu Kaisen Chapter 221, released on April 23, 2023. Gojo says it when asked whether he would lose to Sukuna.
