Mushoku Tensei: Jobless Reincarnation Season 3 is airing, and if you've been waiting since Season 2 wrapped, the wait is over. The season premiered July 4-5, 2026, and as of this week the English dub is live too. Here's everything you need: the actual release schedule, what's aired so far, and where to legally watch it depending on where you live.

## Release Schedule

Season 3 launched with a double-episode premiere, then settled into a weekly Sunday cadence.

| Episode | Release Date (Japan) | Notes |
|---------|----------------------|-------|
| Episode 1 & 2 | July 4-5, 2026 | Launch special, released together |
| Episode 3 | July 12, 2026 | First episode on the regular weekly slot |
| Episode 4 | July 19, 2026 | Weekly Sunday release |
| Episode 5+ | Every Sunday after | 12:00 AM JST in Japan |

New episodes go up weekly at midnight JST. For international viewers, Crunchyroll's confirmed release window from Episode 3 onward is:

- **11:00 AM ET**
- **8:00 AM PT**
- **4:00 PM BST** (UK, summer time)
- **5:00 PM CEST** (Central Europe, summer time)
- **1:00 AM AEST** (Australia, the following day, since Australia doesn't observe daylight saving in July)

Double-check the exact time on Crunchyroll's own listing close to Sunday, since daylight saving offsets shift a couple of times a year and Crunchyroll occasionally adjusts the slot by an hour.

## The English Dub Just Dropped

If you were holding out for the dub instead of subs, it premiered **July 18, 2026** on Crunchyroll at 8:00 AM PT, and it's now on the same weekly schedule as the sub.

Returning cast for Season 3:

- **Madeleine Morris** as Rudeus
- **Lindsay Seidel** as Eris
- **Rachel Robinson** as Ghislaine
- **Bradley Gareth**, **Katie Otten**, and **Cory Koehler** in supporting roles

Jeremy Inman is back as voice director, so the tone and direction match Seasons 1 and 2, with no recast shakeups to worry about.

## Where to Watch

**Crunchyroll** is the main platform for Season 3, sub and dub, and it's where the simulcast actually airs first. Current plans:

- Fan: $9.99/month
- Mega Fan: $13.99/month
- Ultimate Fan: $17.99/month

If you're in the US, Crunchyroll's catalog, including this season, is also reachable through the Hulu app once you link your Crunchyroll subscription, if you'd rather not juggle a separate app.

**A quick Netflix note, because it comes up a lot:** Netflix added Mushoku Tensei to its catalog in April 2026, but that's *Seasons 1 and 2 only*, and only in select territories (UK, Canada, Australia, Germany, France, and a handful of others). The US wasn't included, and Season 3 isn't on Netflix anywhere. Crunchyroll has it exclusively for now.

**If you're in Southeast Asia or South Asia:** Muse Asia streams the sub for free on YouTube, simulcast with Japan, in regions including Indonesia, Malaysia, Bangladesh, Nepal, Cambodia, Laos, Myanmar, Brunei, and Bhutan. It's the real deal, Muse Asia is a licensed distributor, not a bootleg reupload. It is geo-restricted though, so it won't load outside those regions.
