The Kakashi dying meme is a single manga panel of Kakashi Hatake looking completely spent. His headband is on, the mask is up, there are bruises and scratches across his face, and his eyes are half shut. He looks like a man who has given everything and has nothing left. People search for it as "Kakashi dying meme" and, just as often, "Kakashi dieing meme," which is the spelling our template uses because that is how a lot of people type it.

You put a caption next to him that describes something that drained you. That's the whole joke. It works because almost every Naruto fan knows Kakashi is the calm, overpowered teacher, so seeing him this wrecked lands harder than it would with a random character.

## Who Kakashi is, for the non-Naruto crowd

Kakashi was created by Masashi Kishimoto and first shows up in chapter 3 of the *Naruto* manga, back in 1999. He is the leader of Team 7, the squad made up of Naruto, Sasuke and Sakura. He wears a mask over most of his face, reads the *Icha Icha* novels in the middle of fights, and carries a Sharingan eye he received from his teammate Obito. Later in the story he becomes the Sixth Hokage.

He is also one of the most popular characters in the series. According to his [Wikipedia entry](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Kakashi_Hatake), he has placed in the top five of every official *Weekly Shōnen Jump* popularity poll for the series and has taken first place several times. That fan base is a big reason his face gets used for reaction images so often.

*Naruto* itself ran in *Weekly Shōnen Jump* from September 1999 to November 2014 and filled 72 volumes, according to [Wikipedia's Naruto page](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Naruto). So there is a lot of Kakashi material to pull from.

## Why it's called the Kakashi dying meme

The name points at the one time Kakashi really did run out of gas. During Pain's assault on the Hidden Leaf Village, Kakashi fights until he has burned through all of his energy and dies. Pain later uses the last of his own power to bring back the people he killed in that battle, and Kakashi is one of them.

Fans remember that part well. The strongest teacher in the village didn't go out to one big finishing blow. He just ran out. Anyone who has finished a long shift, a final exam or a week of bad sleep knows the feeling, and that is what the template carries.

The panel itself is a black and white close-up from the manga. It reads as tired, beaten and quietly resigned all at the same time, which makes it very easy to caption.

## How people use the Kakashi dying meme

Most captions follow one of a few patterns.

**The "after one small task" joke.** The caption describes something minor, and Kakashi's face does the exaggeration. "Me after replying to two emails." "Me after walking up one flight of stairs." The gap between how small the task is and how destroyed he looks is the punchline.

**The "I gave it everything" joke.** This one is closer to the source. Something genuinely draining happened and the meme says it honestly. "Me after the group project where I did everyone's part." "Retail workers on December 26."

**The labeled version.** Some people put a label on Kakashi and a second label in the empty space beside him. Kakashi becomes "my phone at 2% battery" or "my last brain cell during the exam," and the side text explains what is about to happen.

**The fan in-joke.** Naruto fans use it for story moments, like Kakashi after training Team 7, or Kakashi after reading one more chapter of a long arc.

The blank space on the left side of the panel is handy. You can drop your text there without covering his face, which matters, because the face is the joke.

## Other Naruto meme formats people use

The Kakashi panel sits inside a much larger pile of Naruto memes. A few are worth knowing.

### The Naruto run

This is the most famous one outside the fandom. Characters in the anime run with their arms stretched straight back and their bodies leaning forward. In 2019, the Facebook event "Storm Area 51" joked that if people did the Naruto run, they could move faster than the bullets. According to [Wikipedia's write-up on the event](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Storm_Area_51), it was posted on June 27, 2019, and had about 2 million people marked as "going" by late August. Only around 150 people actually showed up at the gates, and none got in.

### Kakashi reading Icha Icha

Kakashi with his nose in an *Icha Icha* novel while a fight or a lesson happens around him is a natural reaction image. It suits anyone who stays calm and checked out while everything else is on fire.

## Tips for a good Kakashi caption

- Keep it short. One line usually beats two. The panel already says "I'm done."
- Be specific. "Me after work" is fine. "Me after a 9-hour shift where the printer jammed six times" is funnier.
- Don't cover his eyes. The half-closed eyes do most of the work.
- Use the empty side space for your text so the image stays clean.
- If your joke is about something tiny, say how tiny it was. That contrast is where the laugh comes from.

## FAQ

### What is the Kakashi dying meme?

It's a reaction meme built on a manga panel of Kakashi Hatake looking bruised and exhausted. People caption it with something that wore them out, from a hard week to replying to a single email.

### Does Kakashi actually die in Naruto?

Yes, for a while. He dies during Pain's attack on the Hidden Leaf after using up all his energy, then Pain revives him along with the others killed in that battle.

### Why is it spelled "Kakashi dieing" sometimes?

It's a common misspelling of "dying" that stuck to the meme in searches and file names. Both spellings refer to the same tired Kakashi panel.
